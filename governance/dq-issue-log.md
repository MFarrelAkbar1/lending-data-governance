# DQ Issue Log

Every issue below was found by `profiling/profile_report.py` (see `profiling/output/profile.md` and `findings.csv`), by a dbt test, or while building the catalog. Row counts are from the 50,000-application sample loaded on 2026-10-25. "Rule" is the `rule_id` in the dbt tests; the scorecard tracks each rule on raw and on staging.

Treatment values: **cleansed** (value replaced in staging, original kept in raw), **flagged** (row kept, boolean flag column added), **accepted** (documented, no change), **rule fixed** (the rule was wrong, not the data). Rows are never deleted.

## How an issue moves through the pipeline (example DQ-001)

1. **Discovery**: profiling flags DAYS_EMPLOYED max value 365243 repeating in 17.99% of rows.
2. **Rule**: `raw_application_train__days_employed_sign` (validity, warn) and `stg_applications__days_employed_sign` (validity, error) share rule_id APP_DAYS_EMPLOYED_VALID.
3. **Treatment**: `clean_days()` sets it to NULL and `days_placeholder_flag()` sets `is_days_employed_placeholder`.
4. **Monitoring**: the scorecard shows the rule as warn on raw (8,996 failures) and pass on staging on every run.
